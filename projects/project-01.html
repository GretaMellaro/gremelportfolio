UX Design

Energy Provider App

Exploring the value of a dedicated digital experience

Services
Workshop · Prototyping · User Research
Industry
Utilities
Role
Service Designer
Objective
Assess the feasibility and user interest in a dedicated mobile app for an Italian Utility Provider through a human-centred approach based on user research, co-design and iterative prototyping.
01

User Research

The project started with a mixed-method research phase to understand customers' digital behaviours, needs and expectations when interacting with utility services. Insights were gathered through a large-scale telephone survey involving more than 1,300 customers, complemented by in-person interviews at customer service branches.

Highlights
  • Defined research objectives and interview scripts
  • Conducted a large-scale telephone survey (1,300+ participants, 800+ answers)
  • Carried out contextual interviews at customer service branches
  • Analysed quantitative and qualitative findings
  • Mapped digital behaviours and preferred interaction channels
  • Identified recurring needs and opportunities
02

Co-design Workshops

Three collaborative workshops brought together business stakeholders, technical teams and designers to align on the future vision of the app, define priorities and co-create its core functionalities.

Highlights
  • Shared research findings and facilitated ideation sessions
  • Presented and refined the first interactive prototype
  • Prioritised features and defined the MVP together with stakeholders
03

UX/UI Design

The application was designed around users' most frequent tasks while remaining consistent with the company's design system and visual identity. A high-fidelity interactive prototype was developed in Figma.

Highlights
  • Conducted competitive benchmarking
  • Designed user flows and information architecture
  • Built a modular interactive prototype
  • Optimised usability, accessibility and visual hierarchy
04

User Testing

The prototype was validated through usability testing sessions with real customers, collecting behavioural insights and identifying opportunities for refinement.

Highlights
  • Moderated usability testing sessions
  • Evaluated key features including billing, payments and appointment booking
  • Identified usability issues and recurring interaction patterns
  • Iterated the prototype based on research findings
05

MVP Definition

Research and testing outcomes informed the definition of the Minimum Viable Product and a shared implementation roadmap for business and technical stakeholders.

Highlights
  • Prioritised features for phased releases
  • Produced functional requirements documentation
  • Defined ownership and implementation roadmap
  • Presented recommendations and next steps
