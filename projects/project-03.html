Service & UX Design

Interactive Bill

Transforming a static document into an interactive digital experience

Services
UX/UI Design · Information Architecture · Project Management
Industry
Utilities
Role
Service Designer
Objective
Design an Interactive Utility Bill for an Italian Utility Provider, combining user experience, accessibility and sustainability into a single digital service.
01

Experience Design

Designed the interactive experience, information architecture and content strategy to make billing information easier to understand and navigate.

Highlights
  • Designed user flows and interface components
  • Developed content and microcopy
  • Ensured accessibility and readability
  • Created an interactive prototype centred around customers' key tasks
02

Design Iteration

Collaborated closely with technical suppliers and internal teams to validate technical feasibility and continuously refine the experience.

Highlights
  • Coordinated with external development partners
  • Validated technical feasibility
  • Refined the experience through iterative design
  • Ensured consistency across communication and UX
03

Launch Preparation

Prepared the launch strategy by designing onboarding communications and supporting a controlled pilot release before the full rollout.

Highlights
  • Designed multi-channel communication materials
  • Reviewed customer portal and chatbot touchpoints
  • Validated educational content and explainer videos
  • Planned the pilot rollout
  • Designed onboarding communications
04

Customer Feedback

After launch, customer feedback was collected through a telephone survey to evaluate adoption and identify opportunities for future improvements.

Highlights
  • Designed the outbound survey script
  • Analysed qualitative customer feedback
  • Presented findings and recommendations
  • Defined improvements for future iterations
