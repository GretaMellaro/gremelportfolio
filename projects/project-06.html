Service & Product Design

Glide Service

Reimagining urban mobility through speculative design

Services
Speculative Design · Service Design · Product Design · Future Scenario Design
Industry
Mobility
Team
Shaojing Dong, Greta Mellaro, Leonardo Nevola, Elisa Pessina, Alessio Rigamonti
Objective
Develop a future mobility ecosystem that responds to the environmental and social challenges of 2086. Starting from a speculative climate scenario, the project explores how product, service and infrastructure design can create safer, more sustainable and human-centred ways of moving through the city.
01

Future Scenario

The project began by developing a plausible future scenario based on the long-term consequences of climate change, investigating how environmental transformations could reshape cities, mobility and everyday behaviours.

Highlights
  • Explored future environmental and social scenarios
  • Investigated the impact of climate change on urban mobility
  • Identified emerging user needs and mobility challenges
  • Defined the design vision for 2086: a catastrophic scenario where cities develop underground because of pollution
02

System Design

Rather than designing a single product, Glide was conceived as an integrated mobility ecosystem for the underground city combining infrastructure, digital services and wearable technology.

Highlights
  • Designed the mobility service
  • Integrated physical and digital touchpoints
  • Developed interactions between users, infrastructure and AI
03

Product & Infrastructure

The service combines an adaptive wearable sole, an intelligent transport infrastructure and an underground magnetic levitation network, enabling users to walk, slide or levitate depending on distance and environmental conditions.

Highlights
  • Designed the adaptive Glide sole
  • Developed intelligent mobility stations
  • Integrated magnetic levitation technology into the service
  • Designed AI-supported traffic and safety management
Outcome

Recognised at the Guggenheim Bilbao

Glide demonstrates how speculative design can be used to explore future challenges and generate tangible design strategies. The project was selected for the exhibition "Motion. Autos, Art, Architecture" curated by Norman Foster at the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao, showcasing innovative visions for the future of mobility.
