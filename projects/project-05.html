Service Design

Nostra

Co-designing a student-led canteen to encourage sustainable food habits and community building

Services
Service Design · Co-design · Workshop Facilitation
Industry
Food Service · Education
Team
Aurora Chiaravalloti, Gaia Drago Cordaro, Li Guoliang, Greta Mellaro
Objective
Design a plant-based canteen for Politecnico di Milano that promotes sustainable eating through participation, transforming students from passive users into active contributors of the service.
01

Research & Discovery

The project started by exploring students' eating habits, perceptions of plant-based diets and expectations towards university dining services. The research aimed to identify opportunities for creating a more sustainable and engaging food experience.

Highlights
  • Investigated students' attitudes towards plant-based diets through an online survey (100+ participants)
  • Analysed behaviours, motivations and barriers
  • Identified opportunities for community-driven participation
  • Defined key user needs and service requirements
02

Co-design Workshops

Two collaborative workshops were organised with Politecnico students from different academic backgrounds and dietary habits to co-create the future service and gather qualitative insights.

Highlights
  • Facilitated co-design sessions with design and engineering students
  • Defined service roles, responsibilities and interactions
  • Co-created the reward system and participation model
  • Established the shared vision and core values of the service
03

Service Design

Insights gathered during research and workshops informed the design of a participatory service ecosystem, where students actively contribute to the operation of the canteen while developing practical skills and strengthening the campus community.

Highlights
  • Designed the end-to-end service ecosystem
  • Developed the membership and participation model
  • Defined user journeys and service touchpoints
  • Structured incentives including meal discounts, certifications and community activities
04 — Final Concept

Nostra

Nostra reimagines the university canteen as a collaborative space where food becomes a catalyst for sustainability, learning and social connection. Beyond providing affordable plant-based meals, the service encourages responsible consumption, shared ownership and a stronger sense of community across campus.
