Master's Thesis

Enhancing Usability in Metro Stations

Evaluating and improving Milan's metro stations through a human-centred approach

Services
Service Design · UX Research · Usability Testing · Design Strategy
Industry
Public Transport
Role
Researcher & Designer
Objective
Investigate how human-centred design can improve the usability of existing metro stations by identifying users' needs, behaviours and pain points, ultimately translating research findings into practical and scalable design recommendations for Milan's Metro Line 1.
More here
01

Research & Discovery

The project began with an extensive literature review exploring usability, accessibility and user experience in public transport, followed by mixed-method research combining quantitative and qualitative approaches to understand how people experience Milan's Metro Line 1.

Highlights
  • Conducted literature review on usability and public transport
  • Designed and distributed an online survey (207 participants)
  • Planned and moderated in-context usability testing sessions (5 users)
  • Observed real user behaviours across different metro stations
  • Investigated comfort, accessibility, safety and wayfinding
02

Research Analysis

Survey findings and usability testing were analysed together to identify recurring patterns, critical pain points and opportunities for intervention across the entire passenger journey.

Highlights
  • Mapped user journeys from real observations
  • Identified recurring usability issues across stations
  • Analysed quantitative and qualitative findings
  • Synthesised research into actionable design insights
  • Prioritised opportunities according to user impact and feasibility
03

Design Framework

Rather than redesigning individual stations, the project focused on developing a practical framework to support the continuous improvement of existing public transport infrastructure.

Highlights
  • Defined a usability evaluation framework for metro stations
  • Developed design principles grounded in research evidence
  • Organised recommendations into key usability categories
  • Balanced user needs with realistic implementation constraints
04

Designing Usability

The outcome is Designing Usability, a practical guide containing fifteen scalable design interventions addressing wayfinding, accessibility, comfort, interaction and perceived safety.

Highlights
  • Designed 15 evidence-based design recommendations
  • Produced visual scenarios illustrating each intervention
  • Developed a scalable toolkit for designers and transport operators
  • Proposed incremental improvements applicable to existing stations
05 — Impact

Outcome

The project demonstrates how service design and UX research methodologies can complement engineering and architectural approaches, helping public transport operators create infrastructures that are not only efficient, but also intuitive, inclusive and enjoyable to navigate.

A human-centred framework for evaluating and improving metro station usability, providing practical guidance for future renovation projects and public transport design initiatives.
