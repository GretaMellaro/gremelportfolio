Interior & Product Design

Geophony

Designing a co-living experience that reconnects people with nature and community

Services
Interior Design · Product Design · Experience Design · User Journey Design
Industry
Hospitality · Co-living
Team
Greta Mellaro, Max Park, Veronica Piunti, Augusto Savarese
Objective
Design a shared living experience within the historic setting of Villa Arconati, transforming the space into a human-centred co-living environment that promotes wellbeing, creativity and meaningful social interactions through interior, product and experience design.
01

Human-Centred Research

The project began by exploring human needs, wellbeing and the role of shared environments in everyday life. Research focused on understanding how spatial design could encourage self-care, creativity and stronger community relationships.

Highlights
  • Investigated human needs and wellbeing frameworks
  • Identified opportunities to foster social interaction and relaxation
  • Analysed Villa Arconati's historical and architectural identity
  • Defined the design vision around wellbeing and community
02

Experience Strategy

Based on the research, we developed the concept Geophony — an immersive environment where nature, architecture and community coexist in harmony. The experience encourages residents to slow down, reconnect with themselves and share meaningful moments with others.

Highlights
  • Developed the Geophony concept
  • Defined the experience principles and service vision
  • Connected the Villa's heritage with contemporary living
  • Designed activities centred on creativity, wellbeing and community
03

Spatial & Product Design

Working in an international multidisciplinary team, we designed the shared spaces and custom furniture to support different moments of the residents' daily lives — from socialising and creative work to relaxation and quiet reflection.

Highlights
  • Designed the co-living masterplan
  • Developed bespoke furniture and interior solutions
  • Created flexible spaces for work, reading and social interaction
  • Balanced functionality, atmosphere and emotional experience
04

User Journey

The spatial experience was designed as a sequence of moments that gradually transition residents from the outside world into a calm, collaborative environment inspired by nature.

Highlights
  • Designed the end-to-end resident journey
  • Structured spaces around different emotional states
  • Created immersive environments through light, sound and materials
  • Encouraged spontaneous interactions and shared experiences
Outcome

A new vision of co-living

Geophony proposes a new vision of co-living where interior design goes beyond functionality, becoming a tool to support wellbeing, creativity and community. By integrating architecture, furniture and experience design, the project transforms Villa Arconati into a place where people can reconnect with nature, themselves and each other.
