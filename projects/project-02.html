Service & UX Design

Customer Portal Redesign

Simplifying the digital customer experience

Services
User Research · User Testing · Prototyping
Industry
Utilities
Role
Service Designer
Objective
Redesign the customer portal of an Italian Utility Provider to improve accessibility, clarity and ease of use, making essential services easier to find and complete.
01

Discovery & Alignment

The project began with stakeholder interviews and collaborative workshops to understand business objectives, user pain points and opportunities for improvement.

Highlights
  • Facilitated collaborative workshops
  • Mapped the existing customer journey (AS-IS)
  • Identified usability issues and priority areas
  • Focused on contracts, billing and customer support services
02

Research & AS-IS Analysis

Conducted exploratory research and analysed the existing customer portal to understand user behaviours and identify friction across key journeys.

03

Co-design & Future Experience

Worked with stakeholders and internal teams to redesign the information architecture and key user journeys through collaborative design sessions.

Highlights
  • Facilitated co-design workshops
  • Redesigned the information architecture
  • Simplified core customer journeys
  • Introduced contextual guidance and feedback throughout the experience
04

Prototyping & Validation

Designed and tested high-fidelity prototypes through an iterative process, continuously refining the experience based on user feedback.

Highlights
  • Designed wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • Conducted qualitative usability testing
  • Iterated solutions based on research insights
